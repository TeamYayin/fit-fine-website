You stimulate muscular adaptation in the gym; you build muscle and burn fat while recovering. Chronic overtraining suppresses the endocrine axis and degrades parasympathetic tone.

## The Pillars of Systemic Recovery

1. **Parasympathetic Tone (Heart Rate Variability - HRV):** Incorporate breathwork, sauna, or cold exposure to stimulate vagal nerve tone.
2. **Nutrient Repletion:** Ensure baseline caloric and micronutrient needs (magnesium, zinc, vitamin D3) are satisfied on non-training days.
3. **Active Recovery:** Light walking or zone-2 mobility boosts lymphatic clearance without adding muscular micro-trauma.
