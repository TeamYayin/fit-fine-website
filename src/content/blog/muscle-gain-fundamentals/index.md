Hypertrophy is an energy-expensive process requiring deliberate synchronization between mechanical tension and systemic nutrient surplus. Without both elements in harmony, lean contractile accumulation will stall.

## 1. The Anabolic Surplus

Building tissue requires energy beyond maintenance. We advise an intelligent surplus:

* **Novice / Intermediate:** +250 to +400 kcal above maintenance (approx. 0.25–0.5 kg gain per month).
* **Advanced Lifter:** +150 to +250 kcal (prioritizing pure contractile mass with minimal adipose spillover).

## 2. Weekly Training Volume Benchmark

Empirical research establishes an optimal hypertrophy volume range of **10 to 20 hard working sets per muscle group per week** within 1–3 reps in reserve (RIR).

> "Volume that fails to elicit mechanical tension near failure is simply junk volume generating fatigue without adaptation."
