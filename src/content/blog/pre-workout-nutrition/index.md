Pre-workout nutrition dictates acute muscle glycogen availability, muscular intracellular hydration, and central nervous system alertness.

## The Optimal Nutrient Window

* **2–3 Hours Prior:** Whole food meal containing 30–40g of lean protein and 40–60g of low-to-medium GI carbohydrates with minimal dietary fiber and fat.
* **45–60 Minutes Prior:** Fast-acting carbohydrate source (such as ripe bananas, cream of rice, or dates) paired with 500ml water and electrolytes.
