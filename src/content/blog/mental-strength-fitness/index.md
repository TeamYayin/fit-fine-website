Plateaus are not biological dead-ends; they are systemic indicators that your previous baseline has consolidated and a new training stimulus or deload is required.

## Breaking Mental Resistance

1. **Reframing Discomfort:** Treat physical strain as voluntary cognitive resilience training.
2. **Data Over Emotion:** Evaluate body composition through bi-weekly moving averages rather than single-day scale fluctuations.
3. **Periodized Mindset:** Acknowledge that peak performance cycles must be punctuated by restorative cycles.
