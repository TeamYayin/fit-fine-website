Even a 2% decrement in cellular hydration precipitates a 10% to 15% reduction in maximal force production, slows reaction time, and impairs core thermoregulation.

## Optimal Hydration Protocol

* **Daily Baseline:** 35ml to 45ml of purified water per kilogram of body weight.
* **Intra-Workout Mineral Balance:** Supplement with 500mg sodium and 200mg potassium per hour of vigorous transpiration.
* **Morning Rehydration:** Begin each day with 500ml water and a pinch of unrefined sea salt to reactivate adrenal fluid balance.
